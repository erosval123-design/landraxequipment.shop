Questions

About the HY10C Mini Excavator

Will it fit through a single gate?

At 780 mm closed the HY10C clears nearly every single residential gate. Always measure the narrowest point — gate post to gate post or to a step — before delivery.

How do I transport it?

900 kg fits comfortably on a single-axle equipment trailer rated for 2,000 lb. A standard pickup-truck tow package is usually enough — verify your tow rating first.

Is it powerful enough to run a breaker?

Yes — 16 MPa system pressure and 19 L/min flow is enough for a class-appropriate demolition hammer. Confirm the breaker flow requirements before ordering.

What is the warranty?

2 years on parts and 1 year on the engine. We hold spares in stock; see the warranty policy page for the full terms.
