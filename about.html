About us

LandRaxx

LandRaxx is a Brooklyn-based supplier of mini excavators and compact construction equipment, serving contractors, landscapers, utility crews, and owner-operators across the contiguous United States.

We source quality compact machines and ship them directly to job sites — no dealer markup, no showroom overhead. Every machine we list is in stock and ready to ship.

Our team understands what contractors need: accurate specs, honest pricing, and equipment that actually shows up. We back every machine with a 2-year warranty and offer free continental US freight delivery.
