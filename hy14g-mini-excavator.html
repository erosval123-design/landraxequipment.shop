Questions

About the HY14G Mini Excavator

Will it fit through a standard gate?

With the chassis retracted to 800 mm it passes through most residential side gates. Measure the narrowest point on your route — usually the gate post, not the gate leaf.

How do I transport it to site?

1,400 kg operating weight fits on a gooseneck or equipment trailer rated for 3,000 lb or more. Always check your trailer plate and tow vehicle rating before loading.

What attachments are included?

Ships with its standard digging bucket. Breakers, augers, log grabbers, rakes and additional buckets are available separately — ask us to include them in your order.

Do I need an operator licence?

No federal licence is required to own or operate one on private property. Commercial jobsites typically require an NCCO or OSHA-10 certification — a site requirement, not a federal law.
