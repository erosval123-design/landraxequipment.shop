Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Free Shipping

All orders ship free to the contiguous United States (48 states). We do not currently ship to Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, or US territories.

Shipping Method & Carrier

Heavy equipment is shipped via flatbed freight or enclosed trailer depending on the machine. After your order is confirmed, our logistics team will contact you within 1–2 business days to schedule a delivery appointment and confirm the carrier.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your machine ships.

Estimated Transit Times

  • East Coast & Midwest: 5–10 business days
  • West Coast & Mountain States: 7–14 business days
  • All other continental US: 7–14 business days

Transit times are estimates and may vary due to carrier capacity, weather, or regional factors. LandRaxx is not responsible for carrier-caused delays.

Delivery Requirements

Delivery of heavy equipment typically requires a forklift, front-end loader, or loading dock at the destination. Please ensure your delivery site is accessible for a freight vehicle. If you need special arrangements, contact us before ordering.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you will receive tracking information by email. For order status inquiries, contact us at sales@landraxequipment.shop or call +1 800 526 3729.

Damaged Shipments

Inspect all deliveries carefully before signing the delivery receipt. If you notice visible damage, note it on the carrier's delivery form and contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Damage reported after 48 hours may not be eligible for a carrier claim.
