Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

30-Day Return Window

We offer a 30-day return policy. You may request a return within 30 days of receiving your machine. After 30 days, we are unable to accept returns.

Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for a return, your machine must:

  • Be in the same condition as when received — unused or minimally used, with no damage beyond normal inspection
  • Include all original accessories, documentation, and packaging where applicable
  • Not have been modified, altered, or used commercially beyond initial inspection

Non-Returnable Items

  • Machines that have been used commercially or beyond initial inspection/testing
  • Items damaged by misuse, neglect, or improper operation
  • Machines with missing components, altered serial numbers, or unauthorized modifications

How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, contact us at sales@landraxequipment.shop with:

  • Your order number
  • The reason for the return
  • Photos of the machine's current condition

We will respond within 2 business days with return instructions and a Return Authorization (RA) number. Returns without a valid RA number will not be accepted.

Return Shipping Costs

If the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, defective machine), we cover return shipping costs in full. For all other returns, the customer is responsible for return freight costs. Heavy equipment return freight typically costs $300–$800 depending on location.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned machine, we will notify you of the refund decision within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping costs (if any) are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

If you wish to exchange your machine for a different model, please initiate a return and place a new order. We do not process direct exchanges.

Warranty Claims

All machines come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Warranty claims are separate from returns — contact us for warranty service instructions.
